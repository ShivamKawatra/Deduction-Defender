Retailer Policy Reference

Policy A: Promotional deductions require signed program approval and supporting documentation.
Policy B: Display fee reimbursements are not valid unless included in approved allowance schedules.
Policy C: Markdown support is only approved under a written exception approval.
Policy D: Damaged product claims require receipt exception, inspection records, or photo evidence.
Policy E: Deductions outside the approved time window are rejected unless approved in writing.

Compliance standard:
All deduction disputes must include at least one contract reference, one shipment reference, and one supporting evidence note.
